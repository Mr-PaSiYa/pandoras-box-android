## Design Context

### Users
Android users saving online video or audio, including people who want a quick paste-and-download flow and people managing queues, formats, and playlists.

### Brand Personality
Clear, compact, and capable. The interface should feel dependable during long-running downloads and unobtrusive when the user wants to act quickly.

### Aesthetic Direction
Native Android UI with readable dark, light, and AMOLED themes and a user-selected accent. Keep surfaces restrained, controls easy to tap, and information density intentional.

### Design Principles
- Keep the primary action and essential status visible on small phones.
- Show complete information when the user requests detail; scroll long content instead of silently truncating it.
- Group related content with spacing and typography, not extra decoration.
- Respect the selected theme and accent and preserve legible contrast.
